How Walton works

From strategic question to practical decision.

01

Understand

We begin with the client’s situation, objectives and strategic question.

02

Diagnose

We identify what is known, what is uncertain and what needs to be understood before a meaningful decision can be made.

03

Build the Evidence

Walton handles what it can directly and, where appropriate, works with the client’s existing advisers or appropriate specialist expertise.

04

Integrate & Advise

We bring the evidence together, assess the implications and translate it into practical strategic choices and next steps.

Client-first approach

The right expertise, at the right level, for the right decision.

Walton’s role is to protect the client’s strategic interest. Where specialist expertise is required, we consider the most appropriate and economically optimal way to obtain it.

This may involve Walton’s specialist network, the client’s existing advisers, or another specialist chosen by the client. The proposed approach is agreed with the client before external work is commissioned.

Walton keeps the wider strategic question in view and integrates the resulting evidence into coherent advice.

A conversation, not a catalogue
