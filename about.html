About Mladen Janev

Mladen Janev is a hospitality and tourism professional with more than 30 years of international experience across hotels, destination resorts, tourism and the wider hospitality environment.

His experience spans commercial reporting and performance analysis, revenue support, sales, hotel operations, guest experience and destination-resort environments across Europe, North America and Australia.

Working across different parts of the hospitality ecosystem has shaped his understanding of how people, hotels, destinations and technology interact — and how decisions in one area can influence the others.

Walton Hospitality Initiative brings this experience together in an independent advisory practice focused on helping hospitality businesses and destinations understand their circumstances more clearly and make better-informed decisions.

The questions behind Walton

  • Are we creating the experience our guests need and expect?
  • Do our commercial model and business performance support our longer-term objectives?
  • Are our people, systems and technology helping the organisation work well?
  • How does the business relate to the destination and the communities around it?
  • What is changing in the market, and what does that mean for the decisions ahead?

These questions reflect Walton's belief that hospitality is best understood by looking beyond individual measures and considering the relationships that shape the whole experience and business.
