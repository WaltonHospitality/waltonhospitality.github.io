About Mladen Janev

Mladen Janev is a hospitality and tourism professional with more than 30 years of international experience across hotels, destination resorts, tourism and the wider hospitality environment.

His experience includes commercial reporting and performance analysis, revenue support, sales, hotel operations, customer experience and destination-resort environments across Europe, North America and Australia.

Over the course of his career, Mladen has worked across different parts of the hospitality ecosystem, gaining an understanding of how commercial decisions, operational realities, guest expectations and destination dynamics influence one another.

The questions behind Walton

  • How do we create stronger commercial performance?
  • How do we understand the guest we are actually trying to serve?
  • How do hospitality businesses fit within the destinations in which they operate?
  • How can people, data and technology support better decisions without losing the human element of hospitality?

These questions form the thinking behind Walton Hospitality Initiative.

Walton Hospitality Initiative

Walton is being developed as an independent platform for hospitality advisory work, strategic projects, industry collaboration and thought leadership.

The initiative brings together commercial hospitality experience with a broader interest in people, hotels, destinations and technology, and the evolving relationship between hospitality businesses and the places in which they operate.

As Walton develops, the platform will expand to include selected projects, collaborations and further Hospitality Perspectives.
